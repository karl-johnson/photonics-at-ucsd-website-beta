Photonics Society at UC San Diego was founded in 2004. We are a student organization at UCSD which serves as UCSD's [SPIE](https://spie.org) student chapter. We frequently host events for UCSD students and also perform K-12 outreach. In the past few years we've come up with a formula that has worked well for us to be able to do all 3 of the following things simultaneously:

1. Foster an environment for people from the optics & photonics community at UCSD to connect
2. Get people of all ages excited about optics and STEM in general
3. Provide opportunities for hands-on experience in building optical setups / projects

## Our formula

Our most frequent events are our "Optics Hour" events. Essentially, these are monthly events where we order a bunch of free food and show off cool optical demonstrations to students (and staff/faculty) of all backgrounds at UCSD! This is a simple concept but there are multiple important details to this formula which make it extremely effective:

**At least one new demo every event.**
